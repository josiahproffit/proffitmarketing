What areas do you serve?

We deliver and pick up throughout Amelia Island, Fernandina Beach, Yulee, and the surrounding Nassau County area.

What if I need more than a week?

No problem — extend your rental for $49.99 per additional week. Just let us know before your scheduled pickup.

Are the crates sanitized between uses?

Yes. Every crate is cleaned and sanitized after each rental before it goes back out to the next customer.

What happens if a crate is lost or damaged?

A replacement fee applies to any crates that are lost, left behind, or damaged beyond normal wear. We’ll walk through the details when you book.

Can I book same-day?

Same-day delivery may be available depending on our schedule, but we recommend booking at least 48 hours ahead to guarantee your window.
